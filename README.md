# 6th Annual Valise Cup — Live Draft Board

**October 24, 2026 · Laurel Links Country Club · Team USA vs Team Europe**

Open the published site at **https://tkhsecond.github.io/valise-cup-2026/** once GitHub Pages has been enabled.

The entire application—including styles, scripts, team rosters, and the Valise exterior photograph—is contained in **index.html**. There is no build, account login, database, or external service required.

## Enable GitHub Pages

1. Open **Settings → Pages** in this repository.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Select **main** and **/(root)**; then **Save**.
4. After publishing completes, open https://tkhsecond.github.io/valise-cup-2026/.

## Playing formats

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Best Ball | Two-man best ball | 6 | 2 USA vs. 2 Europe |
| Alternate Shot | Two-man alternate shot | 6 | 2 USA vs. 2 Europe |
| Singles | Singles match play | 12 | 1 USA vs. 1 Europe |
| All Matches | Read-only summary | All 24 | — |

Each of the 12 USA golfers and 12 Europe golfers is available once **in each separate round**.

## Operating during the draft

- Select a format tab, click a golfer in the roster, and click their designated team slot to place them. Drag and drop also works.
- The board prevents duplicate assignments in the same round, supports moving and swapping occupied assignments, and has **Undo**.
- For a Mac connected or cast to a widescreen TV, select **TV VIEW** and the fullscreen icon; **All Matches** shows the full 24-match summary.
- All changes save automatically in **this browser and website origin**. They do not sync to other devices.
- Use **More → Download draft backup** for a JSON copy, and **More → Restore a backup** if needed. Make a backup during the draft.
- The browser storing your draft assignments should remain the same throughout the event. Opening this site from a different URL (or a private window) starts a separate saved draft.
